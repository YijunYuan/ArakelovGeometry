The selected Apache-2.0 EtingofRepresentationTheory port (commit 752fe269b3233f08f0795aa4bcdc5b4c84be7a50; five proof modules, 892 lines) proves the actual tensor permutation-centralizer, semisimple image-algebra, and GL-span results. TensorPowerSemisimple specializes the representation-theory input needed in Bost p31, proof of Theorem 1.1: over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field and a positive-rank finite-dimensional E, every actual SL(E) tensor power E^{tensor N}, including N=0, is semisimple as a group-algebra module. It uses the existing determinant normalization to compare GL and SL image algebras at fixed degree, and proves equivariant splitting and invariant lifting for quotients of invariant subspaces. General reductivity and semisimplicity of the infinite group algebra are not claimed. The bounded-polynomial quotient, binary factor regrouping/successive lifts, and actual orbit-ideal separation remain separate producers, so this does not complete A8.
