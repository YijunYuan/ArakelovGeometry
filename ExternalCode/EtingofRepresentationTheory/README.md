# Tensor representation prerequisites

These five proof modules provide algebraic prerequisites for the tensor
instability constructions in Chen–Moriwaki's *Arakelov Geometry over Adelic Curves*.
They prove the permutation centralizer formula, semisimplicity of the tensor
image algebra in characteristic zero, and the general-linear tensor-map span
formula over an infinite field. Detailed representation decompositions and
Lie algebra actions are not included.

The project derives semisimplicity of fixed special-linear tensor powers,
including degree zero, over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field.
It also obtains invariant lifting across equivariant quotients of invariant
subspaces. These statements do not assert semisimplicity of the infinite
special-linear group's group algebra.
