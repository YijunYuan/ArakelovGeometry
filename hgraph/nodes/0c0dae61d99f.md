The map $I_{L/K}:V_L\to\mathbb R^\Omega$ is real-linear. For every
real function $g$ on $\Omega$, one has $I_{L/K}(g\circ\pi_{L/K})=g$.
No global measurability or integrability is required for these cylinder identities.
