Let $K\subseteq F\subseteq L$ be a tower of fields with $L/F$ finite
separable, let $C$ be an algebraically closed extension of $K$, and
let $\tau:F\to C$ be a $K$-embedding. Then
\[
 \#\{\sigma\in\operatorname{Hom}_K(L,C):\sigma|_F=\tau\}=[L:F].
\]
