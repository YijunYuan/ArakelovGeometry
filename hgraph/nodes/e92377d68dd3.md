For a monic polynomial $P=T^m+\sum_{i<m}\mu_iT^i$ over $K$,
a root $a$ in an extension field carrying a nonarchimedean absolute
value satisfies $w(a)\leq M$ whenever $M\geq1$ and
$w(\mu_i)\leq M$ for every $i<m$.
