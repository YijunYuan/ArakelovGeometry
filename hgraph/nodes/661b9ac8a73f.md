For $h:\Omega_L\to[0,\infty]$ put
\[
 I^+_{L/K}h(\omega)=\int_{X_{L,\omega}}h(x)\,dP_{L,\omega}(x).
\]
The integral takes values in $[0,\infty]$. On the common parameter
space of $L$ and $E$, this operator is $I^+_{E/K}$.
