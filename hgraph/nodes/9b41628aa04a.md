title: Finitely many roots from bounded coefficient heights
type: tex
updated: '2026-10-02T13:11:44'

Suppose an adelic structure $S$ on $K$ has the Northcott property.
For any extension field $L/K$, real number $B$, and natural number $D$,
the set of elements of $L$ that are roots of some nonzero polynomial
$F\in K[X]$ of degree at most $D$, with
$h_S^{\mathrm{aff}}(F_j)\leq B$ for every coefficient $F_j$, is finite.
