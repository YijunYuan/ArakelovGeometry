title: Fibre integration respects almost everywhere equality
type: tex
updated: '2026-10-02T11:55:13'

If real functions $f,g$ on $\Omega_L$ are equal $\nu_L$-almost everywhere,
then $I_{L/K}f=I_{L/K}g$ $\nu$-almost everywhere.
