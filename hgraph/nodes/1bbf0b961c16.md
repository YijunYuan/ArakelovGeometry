For a field extension $L/K$ put
\[
 \Omega_L=\{(\omega,w):\omega\in\Omega,\quad
 w\text{ an absolute value on }L,\quad w|_K=\phi_\omega\}.
\]
Write $\pi(\omega,w)=\omega$ and $e_a(\omega,w)=w(a)$ for $a\in L$.
Equip $\Omega_L$ with the smallest sigma algebra making $\pi$ and all $e_a$
measurable. The fibre $X_\omega=\pi^{-1}\{\omega\}$ carries the induced
sigma algebra. A singleton of $\Omega$ need not be measurable; the induced
sigma algebra on $X_\omega$ is nevertheless defined.
