Let $L/K$ be finite, let $w$ be an absolute value on $L$, put
$B=K_{w|_K}$ and $C=B^{\mathrm{ac}}$, and let $u$ extend the norm of
$B$ to $C$. For every $B$-embedding $\tau:L_w\to C$ and every
$x\in L_w$, one has $u(\tau(x))=\lVert x\rVert$.
