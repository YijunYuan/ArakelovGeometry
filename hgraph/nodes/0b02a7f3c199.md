Write $z(t)=\exp(2\pi it)$ and
$T=\{t\in[0,1]:z(t)\text{ is transcendental over }\mathbb Q\}$.
The map $z:\mathbb R\to\mathbb C$ is continuous. Equip $T$ with the
trace Borel $\sigma$-algebra and induced Lebesgue measure.
