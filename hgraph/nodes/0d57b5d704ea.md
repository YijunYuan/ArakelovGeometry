In an algebraic tower $K\subseteq B\subseteq N$, let
$f:\Omega_N\to\mathbb R$ be measurable and $Q_\omega$-integrable.
Then
\[
 \int f\,dQ_\omega
 =I_{B/K}\bigl(I_{N/B}(f\circ e)\bigr)(\omega).
\]
