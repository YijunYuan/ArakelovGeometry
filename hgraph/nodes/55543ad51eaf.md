The sigma algebra $\mathcal A_L$ is generated by the functions
$e_b(\varphi\circ\pi)$, where $b\in L$ and
$\varphi:\Omega\to\mathbb R$ is measurable, with no boundedness
condition on the product or on $\varphi$.
