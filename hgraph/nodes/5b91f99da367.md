For every measurable $\varphi:\Omega\to[0,\infty]$,
\[
 \int_{\Omega_L}\varphi\circ\pi\,d\nu_L=\int_\Omega\varphi\,d\nu.
\]
For every $\nu$-integrable real function $\varphi$, its pullback
$\varphi\circ\pi$ is $\nu_L$-integrable.
