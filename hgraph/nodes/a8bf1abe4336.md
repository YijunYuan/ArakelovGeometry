Assume $S$ has the Northcott property and let $L/K$ be any algebraic
extension. For every $C,\delta\in\mathbb R$,
the set
\[
 \{\alpha\in L:
       h_{S\otimes_K L}^{\mathrm{aff}}(\alpha)\leq C,\ [K(\alpha):K]\leq\delta\}
\]
is finite. In particular this gives Claim 3.5.6 when $L=\overline K$.
