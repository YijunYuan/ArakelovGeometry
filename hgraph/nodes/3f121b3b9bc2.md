For a separable element $c$ over $K$ in an algebraic closure $C$, the
orbit $\{\tau(c):\tau\in\operatorname{Aut}_K(C)\}$ is the set of roots
of the minimal polynomial $P_c$ and has $[K(c):K]$ elements.
