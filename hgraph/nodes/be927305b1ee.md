title: Uniqueness from singleton masses

Suppose $X$ is countable and every singleton is measurable. For each
$m:X\to[0,\infty]$, there is exactly one measure $\mu$ with
$\mu(\{x\})=m(x)$ for all $x$; it is $\mu_m$.
