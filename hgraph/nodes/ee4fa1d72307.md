For $b\in K$, write
$h_S^{\mathrm{aff}}(b)=\int_\Omega\log\max(1,|b|_\omega)\,d\nu(\omega)$.
Then $h_S^{\mathrm{aff}}(b)=h_S(1,b)$ and, if $S$ is proper,
$h_S^{\mathrm{aff}}(b)=h_S([1:b])$.
