Let $L$ be a field, $I$ an index set, and $x=[a]\in\mathbb P(L^I)$.
If $a_j\ne0$, the tuple $b_i=a_i/a_j$ has $b_j=1$, is nonzero,
and represents $x$. If two projective points have equal normalized
tuples, even with respect to different nonzero coordinates, they are equal.
