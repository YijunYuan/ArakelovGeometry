Fix $\omega$ and any extension $v$ of $\phi_\omega$ to an algebraic
closure $C$ of $K$. For every $b\in L$,
\[
 I_{L/K}e_b(\omega)=\frac1{[L:K]}
   \sum_{\sigma:L\hookrightarrow C}v(\sigma(b)).
\]
