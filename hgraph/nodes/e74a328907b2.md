Let $S=(K,(\Omega,\mathcal A,\nu),\phi)$ be an adelic curve and
let $F\subseteq K$ be a subfield. Its restriction to $F$ is the
adelic curve
\[
 S|_F=(F,(\Omega,\mathcal A,\nu),
            (\omega\mapsto\phi_\omega|_F)).
\]
