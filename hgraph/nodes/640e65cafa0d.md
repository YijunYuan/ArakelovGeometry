Let $\alpha:S'\to S$ and $\beta:S''\to S'$ be morphisms of
adelic curves. Then
\[
 \alpha\circ\beta=
 (\beta^\#\circ\alpha^\#,
  \alpha_\#\circ\beta_\#,
  I_\alpha\circ I_\beta)
\]
is a morphism from $S''$ to $S$.
