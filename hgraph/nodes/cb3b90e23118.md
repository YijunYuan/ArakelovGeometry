The adelic extension $S\otimes_K L$ is
$(L,(\Omega_L,\mathcal A_L,\nu_L),\phi_L)$, where $\phi_L(\omega,w)=w$.
The measurable and integrable logarithmic evaluations just proved
make this an adelic structure.
