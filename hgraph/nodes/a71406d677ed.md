Affine heights are nonnegative, $h_S^{\mathrm{aff}}(0)=0$, and
$h_S^{\mathrm{aff}}(b^m)=m h_S^{\mathrm{aff}}(b)$ for every $m\geq0$.
Also $h_S^{\mathrm{aff}}(-b)=h_S^{\mathrm{aff}}(b)$.
