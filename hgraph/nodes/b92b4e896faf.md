For a finite stage $F$ and a real $\nu_F$-integrable function $g$,
the pullback $g\circ p_F$ is $\nu_L$-integrable, its integral equals
$\int g\,d\nu_F$, and its $L^1$ norm equals $\int |g|\,d\nu_F$.
