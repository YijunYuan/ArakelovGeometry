For a real absolute value $v$ on a field and pairwise distinct elements
$\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_d$, where $d\geq1$, one has
\[
 \max_i v(\alpha_i)
 =\limsup_{N\to\infty}v\left(\sum_{i=1}^d\alpha_i^N\right)^{1/N}.
\]
