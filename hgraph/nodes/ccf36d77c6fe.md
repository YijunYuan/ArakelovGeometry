For every real $g:\Omega\to\mathbb R$,
$I_{L/K}(g\circ\pi_{L/K})=g$. For every finite stage $F$ and every
real $h:\Omega_F\to\mathbb R$,
$I_{L/K}(h\circ p_F)=I_{F/K}h$.
