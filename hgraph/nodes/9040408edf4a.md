Let $L/K$ be finite, let $F$ be an intermediate field, and let $w$
be an absolute value on $L$. Then
\[
 [F_{w|_F}:K_{w|_K}]\,[L_w:F_{w|_F}]
 =[L_w:K_{w|_K}].
\]
