For $B\in\mathcal A_L$ define
\[
 \nu_L(B)=\int_\Omega I^+_{L/K}(\mathbf1_B)(\omega)\,d\nu(\omega).
\]
This prescription is a measure on $(\Omega_L,\mathcal A_L)$.
