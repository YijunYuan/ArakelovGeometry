Let $L/K$ be finite, let $F$ be intermediate with $L/F$ separable,
and let $x$ be an absolute value on $F$. Then
\[
 \sum_{w|_F=x}[L_w:K_{x|_K}]
 =[F_x:K_{x|_K}]\,[L:F].
\]
