type: tex
updated: '2026-10-02T11:55:12'

Let $L/K$ be finite, let $w$ be an absolute value on $L$, put
$B=K_{w|_K}$ and $C=B^{\mathrm{ac}}$, and let $u$ extend the norm
of $B$ to $C$. Then
\[
 \#\{\sigma\in\operatorname{Hom}_K(L,C):u\circ\sigma=w\}
 =[L_w:B]_s.
\]
