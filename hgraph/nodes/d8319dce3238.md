Let $L/K$ be algebraic, $\alpha\in L$, let $d=[K(\alpha):K]$, and let
$F=X^d+a_{d-1}X^{d-1}+\cdots+a_0\in K[X]$ be its monic
minimal polynomial. Then
\[
 h_S(F)\leq d\,h_{S\otimes_K L}^{\mathrm{aff}}(\alpha)+(d-1)(\log2)A_S,
 \qquad
 h_S^{\mathrm{aff}}(F_j)\leq h_S(F)\quad(j\geq0).
\]
