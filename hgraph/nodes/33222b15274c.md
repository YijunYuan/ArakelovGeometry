Let $L/K$ be finite, let $w$ be an absolute value on $L$, and put
$v=w|_K$, $B=K_v$ and $C=B^{\mathrm{ac}}$. Let $u$ extend the norm of
$B$ to $C$. Restriction induces a bijection
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_B(L_w,C)\longrightarrow
 \{\sigma\in\operatorname{Hom}_K(L,C):u\circ\sigma=w\}.
\]
