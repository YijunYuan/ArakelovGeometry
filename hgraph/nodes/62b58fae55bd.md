For every adelic curve $S=(K,(\Omega,\mathcal A,\nu),\phi)$,
the triple
\[
 \operatorname{id}_S=
 (\operatorname{id}_K,\operatorname{id}_\Omega,
  \operatorname{id}_{L^1(\Omega,\mathcal A,\nu)})
\]
is a morphism from $S$ to itself.
