For arbitrary algebraic $K\subseteq B\subseteq N$ and every
nonnegative measurable $g$ on the iterated parameter space,
\[
 I^+_{B/K}(I^+_{N/B}g)
 =I^+_{N/K}(g\circ e^{-1})
\]
pointwise on $\Omega_K$.
