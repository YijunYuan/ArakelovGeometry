For every $g:\Omega\to[0,\infty]$, $I^+_{L/K}(g\circ\pi_{L/K})=g$.
For every finite stage $F$ and every $h:\Omega_F\to[0,\infty]$,
$I^+_{L/K}(h\circ p_F)=I^+_{F/K}h$.
