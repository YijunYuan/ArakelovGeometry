Let $L/K$ be finite separable, let $w$ be an absolute value on $L$,
put $B=K_{w|_K}$ and $C=B^{\mathrm{ac}}$, and let $u$ extend the
norm of $B$ to $C$. The number of $K$-embeddings $\sigma:L\to C$
with $u\circ\sigma=w$ equals $[L_w:B]$.
