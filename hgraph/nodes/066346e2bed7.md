If $L/K$ is finite separable and $w$ extends an absolute value $v$ of $K$,
then $L_w/K_v$ is separable. This is the separability assertion in the
proof of Lemma 3.4.2 of \cite{chen-moriwaki}; the following argument uses
a spanning basis in place of the factorization of a primitive element's
minimal polynomial.
