Define $\lambda:\Gamma\to\mathbb R_{\geq0}$ by $\lambda(0)=0$ and
$\lambda(\tau^m)=e^m$. For $\gamma=\tau^m\ne0$, one has
$\log\lambda(\gamma)=m$.
