For a polynomial $P\in K[T]$ of degree $m$, put
$G_P(\omega)=\max_{0\leq i\leq m}\ell_i(\omega)$, where
$\ell_i(\omega)=\log|P_i|_\omega$ if $P_i\ne0$ and
$\ell_i(\omega)=0$ if $P_i=0$.
