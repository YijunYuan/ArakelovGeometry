Fibre integration induces a linear contraction from
$L^1(\Omega_L,\nu_L)$ to $L^1(\Omega,\nu)$.
Precisely, there is a continuous linear map $T$ satisfying
$T[f]=[I_{L/K}f]$ for every integrable real $f$, and $\|T\|\le1$.
