For $\nu_L$-integrable real $f$,
\[
 \int_\Omega|I_{L/K}f|\,d\nu\le\int_{\Omega_L}|f|\,d\nu_L,
 \qquad
 \int_{\Omega_L}f\,d\nu_L=\int_\Omega I_{L/K}f\,d\nu.
\]
