Let $P_{L,\omega}$ be the restriction of $\mu_\omega^*$ to
$\mathcal A_{L,\omega}$. This restriction is a measure, since the
Carath\'eodory measurable sets form a $\sigma$-algebra containing the
cylinder generators.
