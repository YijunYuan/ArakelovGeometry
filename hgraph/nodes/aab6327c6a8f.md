Let $\mu,\nu$ be measures on $(\Omega_L,\mathcal A_L)$ with $\mu$
finite. If $(\pi_{L/F})_*\mu=(\pi_{L/F})_*\nu$ for every finite
intermediate field $F/K$, then $\mu=\nu$.
