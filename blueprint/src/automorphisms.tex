\section{Measurability of automorphism actions}

Let $S=(K,(\Omega,\mathcal A,\nu),\phi)$ be an adelic curve.
We use the intrinsic extension parameter space $\Omega_L$ and the
projective $\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal A_L$ of $S\otimes_K L$.
The conventions follow \cite[Section 3.6]{chen-moriwaki}.

\begin{definition}[Pullback by an automorphism]
\label{def:automorphism-pullback}
\source{chen-moriwaki-adelic:page-0218}
\uses{def:extension-parameters,lem:extension-coordinates}
\lean{ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.aut,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.aut_proj,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.aut_eval,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.aut_refl,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.aut_trans,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.aut_symm_aut,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.aut_aut_symm}
\leanok
For $\tau\in\operatorname{Aut}_K(L)$ define
\[
 T_\tau(\omega,w)=(\omega,w\circ\tau).
\]
Thus $\pi T_\tau=\pi$ and $|a|_{T_\tau x}=|\tau(a)|_x$.
This definition makes sense for every field extension $L/K$.
It satisfies $T_1=1$, $T_\tau T_\sigma=T_{\sigma\circ\tau}$,
and $T_{\tau^{-1}}=T_\tau^{-1}$. In particular it restricts to a
permutation of every fibre. With composition as multiplication on
automorphisms, this source convention is a right action.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
An automorphism preserves sums, products, zero and one and is injective,
so composition with it preserves the absolute-value axioms. For $a\in K$,
$\tau(a)=a$, and hence the restriction condition over $K$ is unchanged.
The base-point and evaluation identities follow from the formula.
For two automorphisms, the absolute value at $a$ after first $T_\sigma$
and then $T_\tau$ is $w(\sigma(\tau(a)))$. This proves the composition
identity. The identity automorphism and inverse identities follow by
evaluation at every $a$. The base coordinate is fixed throughout.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Measurability of automorphism pullback]
\label{prop:automorphism-measurable}
\dcref{Proposition 3.6.1}
\source{chen-moriwaki-adelic:page-0218}
\uses{def:automorphism-pullback,lem:extension-coordinates,prop:projective-measurable-equality,def:algebraic-extension}
\lean{ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.measurable_aut,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.autMeasurableEquiv,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.measurable_aut_projective}
\leanok
Let $L/K$ be algebraic and $\tau\in\operatorname{Aut}_K(L)$.
Then $T_\tau:(\Omega_L,\mathcal A_L)\to(\Omega_L,\mathcal A_L)$
is measurable. Its inverse $T_{\tau^{-1}}$ is also measurable, so it
is a measurable equivalence. The same measurability holds for an
arbitrary extension equipped with the evaluation-generated $\sigma$-algebra.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
By Proposition~\ref{prop:projective-measurable-equality}, $\mathcal A_L$
is generated by the base projection and all evaluations.
Their composites with $T_\tau$ are respectively $\pi$ and
$x\mapsto|\tau(a)|_x$, which are measurable by
Lemma~\ref{lem:extension-coordinates}. Inverse images under $T_\tau$
therefore preserve every generator and hence the generated $\sigma$-algebra.
Applying this argument to $\tau^{-1}$ proves the assertion for the inverse.
The generator argument itself needs no algebraicity; algebraicity is used
to identify this $\sigma$-algebra with that of $S\otimes_K L$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Measurable maximum over an automorphism orbit]
\label{lem:measurable-orbit-maximum}
\source{chen-moriwaki-adelic:page-0218,chen-moriwaki-adelic:page-0219}
\uses{prop:automorphism-measurable,def:automorphism-pullback}
\lean{ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.orbitMaximum,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.apply_aut_le_orbitMaximum,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.orbitMaximum_le,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.exists_aut_eq_orbitMaximum,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.le_orbitMaximum,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.measurable_orbitMaximum,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.orbitMaximum_aut,ArakelovGeometry.AdelicStructure.ParameterSpaceExtension.orbitMaximum_le_of_invariant}
\leanok
Let $L/K$ be finite and let $F:\Omega_L\to\mathbb R$ be measurable.
Then
\[
 \widetilde F(x)=\max_{\tau\in\operatorname{Aut}_K(L)} F(T_\tau x)
\]
is measurable and invariant under every $T_\sigma$. It dominates $F$,
and is the least invariant function dominating $F$. The maximum is
attained. These conclusions do not require normality or separability.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The automorphism group is finite and contains the identity. Indeed, for
finitely many field generators, each image is among the finitely many
roots of its minimal polynomial; their images determine an automorphism.
The displayed maximum is therefore a maximum of a finite nonempty family
of real numbers, and is attained. Each function $F\circ T_\tau$ is
measurable by Proposition~\ref{prop:automorphism-measurable}, so their
finite maximum is measurable. Evaluating at the identity gives
$F\leq\widetilde F$.
For each $\sigma$, the composition identity shows that
$\widetilde F(T_\sigma x)$ takes its maximum over the same orbit:
$\tau\mapsto\sigma\circ\tau$ permutes the automorphism group.
Thus $\widetilde F(T_\sigma x)=\widetilde F(x)$. Finally, if $G$ is
invariant and $F\leq G$, then for every $\tau$ one has
$F(T_\tau x)\leq G(T_\tau x)=G(x)$. Taking the maximum proves
$\widetilde F\leq G$.
\end{proof}
